# FEA_AP-PLY

Open-source FEA pipeline for AP-PLY / pseudo-woven composite simulation, from coupon-scale tensile through high-velocity projectile penetration. Single-tool stack on top of OpenRadioss with all-solid-element discretisation, no GUI, fully scriptable.

## Status

Exploratory / planning phase. Master plan committed; 16 per-stage specifications written; toolchain bring-up complete on a Linux compute host. No stage has been validated end-to-end yet.

## Architecture target

UofSC pseudo-woven (PW) and TU Delft AP-PLY are the same automated-fibre-placement architecture under different names. UofSC also called it *clutch laminate* during 2017-2019. The reference panel for the terminal validation is Vakili Rad et al. 2020 (UofSC, Hexcel IM7G/8552-1, 24-ply, 4.55 mm, 1143 mm × 838 mm). Geometry is generated by a planned standalone Python port of Rutger Kok's Edinburgh AP-PLY preprocessor.

## Test progression (16 stages, single-tool, all solid)

| # | Stage | Standard | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Linear-elastic 3-pt and 4-pt bending of a solid beam | — | tip deflection within 1% of Euler-Bernoulli |
| 2 | Cantilever, large-deflection geometric nonlinearity | — | within 2% of Bisshopp-Drucker 1945 |
| 3 | Isotropic dogbone tension | ASTM E8 | uniform σ within 1%, ε within 1% |
| 4 | Open-hole tension (Kirsch) | ASTM D5766 | K_t within 2% at hole edge |
| 5 | Dogbone with isotropic ductile damage | — | mesh-objective load-deflection within 5% RMS |
| 6 | Dogbone with Tsai-Wu / Hashin / Puck side-by-side | — | failure envelope matches WWFE-II |
| 7 | UD tow tension, on-axis and off-axis | ASTM D3039 | E1, E2, ν12, G12 within 2% of Soden card |
| 8 | Ply-rotation transformation | — | engineering constants vs. analytic within 1% |
| 9 | Cross-ply and quasi-iso laminate (solid plies) | — | A-matrix within 2% of CLT |
| 10 | UD tow-wise direct mesoscale | — | effective E1, E2, G12 within 5% of Halpin-Tsai |
| 11 | Pseudo-woven mesoscale direct (tow + matrix) | — | within 10% of Kok 2022 reported values |
| 12 | DCB and ENF cohesive zone | ASTM D5528, D7905 | DCB peak load within 5% of Camanho-Davila 2003 |
| 13 | Drop-weight LVI | ASTM D7136 | peak force within 10%, delam area within 20% |
| 14 | Compression after impact (CAI), chained | ASTM D7137 | residual strength within 10% |
| 15 | Flat-coupon ballistic | NIJ 0101.06 | V50 within 5%, residual velocity within 10% |
| 16 | Pseudo-woven panel ballistic (project goal) | ASTM D8101 | V50 within 7% of Vakili Rad 2020 |

Each `tests/stage_NN_*/spec.md` contains geometry, mesh, BCs, material card, OpenRadioss deck skeleton, reference solution, validation criterion, and per-stage runner skeleton.
